SynLogic is a comprehensive logical reasoning data synthesis framework that generates diverse, verifiable reasoning data at scale, addressing the critical gap in high-quality logical reasoning training data for developing general reasoning capabilities in Large Language Models (LLMs). It covers 35 diverse logical reasoning tasks, provides controllable difficulty synthesis, and offers verifiable rewards through rule-based verification for RL training.
