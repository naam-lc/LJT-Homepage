C-Eval is a comprehensive Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models. It consists of multi-choice questions spanning 52 diverse disciplines and four difficulty levels, intended to track the progress and analyze the important strengths/shortcomings of foundation models.

**Paper:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.08322](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.08322)
