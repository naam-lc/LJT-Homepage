This paper identifies and studies the perception bottleneck of vision-language models (VLMs) for chart understanding. It provides implementations for training and evaluating CLIP and LLaVA models on chart understanding tasks, including CLIP contrastive learning on chart tasks and LLaVA training/evaluation on multiple chart-related benchmarks.
